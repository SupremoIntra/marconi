# Welcome Marconiani!
## Manifesto for Free Software and Open Knowledge

### Introduction

Welcome to the **Guglielmo Marconi High School Crypto, Networking, and Databases Challenge**! This event is inspired by the visionary principles of Richard Stallman, the founder of the Free Software Movement. Stallman's philosophy emphasizes the importance of software freedom, collaboration, and the ethical use of technology. 

### Our Vision

We believe in a world where software is free and open, where knowledge is shared, and where technology serves the common good. This challenge is designed to foster these values among students, encouraging them to explore the realms of cryptography, networking, and databases through the lens of free software.

### Objectives

- **Promote Free Software**: Encourage the use of free and open-source software in all aspects of the challenge.
- **Foster Collaboration**: Create an environment where students work together, share knowledge, and learn from each other.
- **Enhance Technical Skills**: Provide hands-on experience in cryptography, networking, and databases, preparing students for future technological endeavors.
- **Ethical Technology Use**: Instill a sense of responsibility and ethics in the use of technology, aligning with Stallman's principles.

### Call to Action - NEWS COMING SOON

Join us in this exciting challenge and become part of a movement that values freedom, collaboration, and ethical technology. Let's work together to build a future where software and knowledge are free for all!

**W Free Software!**
